Modern Robotics IMU Fusion

MPU6050 Stability System

Develop a self-balancing robot or inverted pendulum. Utilize the MPU6050 6-DOF IMU to capture accelerometer and gyroscope data, apply complementary filters, and implement cascade PID to maintain vertical equilibrium.

Experiment 3
AGV Robotics Outer-Loop Control

Self-Balancing Scooter System

Open-Ended Problem Statement: Light Cargo AGV Adaptation

A logistics company plans to adapt a self-balancing scooter (Two-Wheeled Inverted Pendulum) as an automated light-cargo delivery robot (AGV) inside a factory. As control engineers, your team is required to characterize the scooter's original dynamic response without modifying its internal circuitry, derive its mathematical model (Transfer Function), and design an outer-loop control system using a microcontroller (Arduino/ESP32) and MPU6050 sensor to minimize pitch angle oscillation and settling time under sudden load disturbances.

  • System Characterization & Transfer Function
  • Outer-Loop Control (Arduino/ESP32 & MPU6050)
  • Minimize Pitch Oscillation & Settling Time
