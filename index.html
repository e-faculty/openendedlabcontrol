Experiment 1
Classical Control PID

Ball and Beam System

Stabilize a rolling ball on a tilting beam using feedback control. This highly nonlinear and unstable system is a classic benchmark for studying PID tuning, steady-state error, and closed-loop transfer functions.

  • Mathematical Modeling & Linearization

  • Real-time Position Tracking

Experiment 2
Modern Robotics IMU Fusion

MPU6050 Stability System

Develop a self-balancing robot or inverted pendulum. Utilize the MPU6050 6-DOF IMU to capture accelerometer and gyroscope data, apply complementary filters, and implement cascade PID to maintain vertical equilibrium.

  • I2C Communication & Sensor Fusion
  • Complementary & Kalman Filtering
  • LQR / Cascade PID Balancing
